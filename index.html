Decision Framing

Keep. Repair. Rebuild.

Not every AI-built app needs a rewrite. The expensive mistake is guessing which path you’re on.

Keep

Stabilize what works

Core flows are sound. Document them, verify them, and avoid accidental churn while you ship carefully.

Repair

Harden the weak spots

Structure is salvageable, but validation, auth, data handling, or tests need focused repair before scale.

Rebuild

Reset on purpose

When foundations won’t carry the product, rebuild with clarity — informed by what the v1 already taught you.
