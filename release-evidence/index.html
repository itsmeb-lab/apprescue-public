Does this read my code or run tests?

No. It is a structured self-assessment that runs in your browser. It never connects to your repository or executes code. “Current evidence reported” means you supplied a reference, a version, an environment, and a recent observation date—not that we verified them.

Why not give me a readiness percentage?

A score can hide an unresolved authorization or recovery failure. This tool keeps the individual gaps visible, and never returns a “safe to ship” result. Its ordering is checklist guidance, not a diagnosis of your app.

What happens to my answers?

The checker keeps answers in this tab's memory only. It uses no analytics, cookies, local storage, external fonts, or API calls. Your host may still record ordinary access logs. Exports stay on your device unless you choose to share them. The separate AppRescue beta form has its own data handling.

Can I mark a check out of scope?

Yes, with a reason. This records your scope decision; it does not verify that the check is genuinely inapplicable. Missing reasons remain visible gaps.

Is this powered by an AI call?

No. The checklist and gap ordering are deterministic. There is no per-result AI request, payment, or subscription. This tool is not evidence that AppRescue's broader assessment or automation pipeline has been deployed.

Can I use it with clients?

Yes. Review your export and share it as a self-reported evidence inventory. Keep the limitations attached. Do not present it as an independent audit or imply that AppRescue verified your software.
