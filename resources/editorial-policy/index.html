Publication rules

Editorial policy

Author: AppRescue. These resource pages are written to be useful first. Partner revenue, if it ever exists, is secondary and cannot change calculator results, page order, or whether a method applies.

Reviewed 13 September 2026. Revision dates change only after a real review of the page or its sources.

What kind of work this is

  • Documents and calculations are based on cited primary documentation and on arithmetic you can re-run. They are not a substitute for opening your own billing screen.
  • Hands-on tests are labeled only when we actually performed them. This cluster does not claim a live vendor account walkthrough, a hosted production deploy, or an independent audit.
  • Examples are hypothetical. Numbered illustrations on the calculator are planning scenarios, not vendor price lists, customer results, or testimonials.

What we will not publish

  • Fake reviews, invented testimonials, or star ratings.
  • Vendor winner lists, “best tool” rankings, or review-schema markup that implies a rated product.
  • Copied commission amounts, tracking parameters, or a claim that AppRescue is an enrolled affiliate.
  • Search-volume, traffic, conversion, or revenue figures we did not measure.

Those limits follow the spirit of Google’s spam policies (scaled unhelpful content, site reputation abuse, and deceptive engagement) and the FTC Endorsement Guides FAQ on material connections. We do not claim those documents certify this site.

Links today

App and tool links on these pages are ordinary unpaid links. There is no accepted affiliate account and no tracked partner URL in this cluster. Non-affiliate paths stay available: official documentation, vendor billing settings, and your own invoice remain the primary references.

If a paid link is added later

A financial relationship cannot influence utility results, ordering, or applicability. If a future link is paid or commission-bearing, the page must say, next to that link: “We may earn a commission if you buy through this link.” The same link must use a sponsored relationship attribute. Unpaid alternatives must remain listed. We do not claim independent financial incentives.

Our own service

Links to AppRescue beta consideration are labeled as our own service. They are not a certification, security approval, or evidence that we verified your application. The existing Release Evidence Check organizes your answers; it does not test your app.

Indexing after publication

A resource sitemap exists at /resources/sitemap.xml. Submitting it, or using Search Console recrawl tools, is a later hosting-owner step. Google’s recrawl documentation states that requesting a crawl does not guarantee inclusion.

This policy is part of a publication candidate. It is not proof that pages are hosted, indexed, or earning anything.
